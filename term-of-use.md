## 9. Privacy

- **Nexa 4 Course Format** stores no personal data.
- **Nexa4 Theme** collects, stores and shares no personal data, and creates no database tables or cookies. It saves two display preferences per user in Moodle's standard user preferences: the colour mode (light or dark) and the sidebar state (expanded or collapsed). They contain no personal information, are included in Moodle's privacy data export, and are removed when the user is deleted.
- **Optional external services.** Depending on your settings, pages may load resources from third parties:
  - **Google Fonts**, when a site font other than "Moodle Font" is selected, which sends the visitor's IP address and browser details to Google. Select "Moodle Font" to avoid this.
